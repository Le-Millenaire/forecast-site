Analysis · Germany · Saxony-Anhalt · September 6, 2026

The AfD three seats short of a majority, facing a parliament with no simple alternative

Final result · source: Landeswahlleiterin Sachsen-Anhalt, September 22, 2026

The essentials in three sentences
  1. The AfD comes first with 43.8% of the vote and 39 of 83 seats, exactly the number our model forecast.
  2. It is three seats short of an absolute majority, set at 42.
  3. Without the AfD, a majority requires bringing together the five other parties elected: 44 seats in all.

The new Landtag

83 seats. The dotted line marks the absolute majority, 42 seats.

Our forecast against the result

Grey bar: our 90% range. Line: our central value. Solid dot: result inside the range. Hollow diamond: result outside the range.

What the model sawThe AfD first, at 39 seats. The right winner in 38 of 41 constituencies, and in all 37 where we rated a favourite at 90% or more.
The surprise of the electionThe CDU falls to 17.2%, below our range. The Greens and the BSW do better than forecast, which brings the BSW into the Landtag with 5 seats.
What nextNo two- or three-party coalition reaches 42 seats without the AfD. Forming a government will depend on whether the other five parties can agree.
